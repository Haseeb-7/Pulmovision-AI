Limitations
  • Dataset dependence. The model reflects the data it was trained on. Details of that dataset are not shown in this interface.
  • Limited generalization. Images from other scanners, hospitals, populations, projections or quality levels may behave very differently.
  • Classification errors. The model can label an image incorrectly in either direction. A high score does not guarantee a correct result.
  • Binary output only. It does not assess other conditions, localize findings, or explain its decision.
  • Downscaled input. Images are resized to 128 × 128 before inference, which discards fine detail.
  • No clinical validation. This prototype has not been clinically validated or approved for any medical use.
Read before use

About This Prototype

PulmoVision AI is a technical demonstration of an image classification pipeline built for learning and experimentation.

What it does

  • Accepts a chest X-ray image (PNG, JPG, JPEG) from your device.
  • Sends it to a local FastAPI backend that resizes it and runs a CNN.
  • Shows the predicted class (NORMAL or PNEUMONIA) and the model's score.
  • Lets you download a plain-text report of that single result.

What it is not

  • Not a medical device or diagnostic service.
  • Not validated for clinical use, triage or treatment decisions.
  • Not a replacement for review by a qualified clinician.
  • Not a record system: no patient data or images are stored by this interface.

Reading a result

  • The class is the model's best guess between two labels.
  • The score is model-reported confidence, not a calibrated probability.
  • Any result may be wrong; do not act on it clinically.
  • If you have a health concern, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
